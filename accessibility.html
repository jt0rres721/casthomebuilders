What We Do

Every Feature, Built for Safety and Style

These aren't clinical add-ons bolted onto an existing bathroom. We design everything in from the start so it looks intentional, beautiful, and custom.

Curbless Walk-In Showers

No lip, no step, no tripping hazard. Curbless showers are safer, easier to clean, and — done right — look more luxurious than a standard shower. We handle the waterproofing, slope, and drain placement so water goes exactly where it should.

Grab Bars & Support Rails

Modern grab bars come in brushed nickel, matte black, chrome — they look like intentional design choices, not medical equipment. We block the walls during framing so bars are anchored to studs and rated for full body weight.

Built-In Shower Benches

A tiled bench inside the shower isn't just for accessibility — it's a feature anyone would want. We build them into the shower structure so they're seamless, watertight, and strong enough to fully bear weight.

Slip-Resistant Flooring

Wet tile floors are the #1 cause of bathroom falls. We install textured tile and stone with a high coefficient of friction — materials that grip even when wet, without looking rough or industrial.

Comfort Height Toilets

Standard toilets sit at 15 inches — comfort height models sit at 17–19 inches, which is dramatically easier on knees, hips, and back. A small change that makes a significant daily difference.

Wider Doorways & Turning Radius

ADA-compliant doorways are 36 inches wide — enough for a walker or wheelchair. We handle the framing, finishing, and any load-bearing considerations so the opening is safe and clean, not a patch job.

Why It Matters

Aging in Place Isn't Just a Trend.
It's What Most Families Actually Want.

Nearly 90% of adults over 65 say they want to stay in their own home as they age. But most homes weren't built with that in mind — and the bathroom is the most dangerous room in the house, responsible for over 235,000 emergency room visits per year in the US.

An accessibility remodel isn't about preparing for the worst. It's about giving your family — and yourself — the freedom to age on your own terms, in the place you love, without fear.

We specialize in this work because we believe the solution should be beautiful. A bathroom that's been thoughtfully adapted for safety shouldn't feel clinical or institutional. It should feel like home.
