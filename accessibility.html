What We Install

Every Feature, Built for Safety and Style

These aren't clinical add-ons bolted onto an existing bathroom. We design everything in from the start so it looks intentional, beautiful, and custom.

Curbless Walk-In Showers

No lip, no step, no tripping hazard. Safer, easier to clean, and — done right — more luxurious than a standard shower. We handle waterproofing, slope, and drain placement so water goes exactly where it should.

Grab Bars & Support Rails

Brushed nickel, matte black, chrome — they look like design choices, not medical equipment. We block the walls during framing so every bar is anchored to studs and rated for full body weight.

Built-In Shower Benches

A tiled bench inside the shower isn't just for accessibility — it's a feature anyone would want. Built into the shower structure so it's seamless, watertight, and strong enough to fully bear weight.

Slip-Resistant Flooring

Wet tile floors are the #1 cause of bathroom falls. We install textured tile and stone that grip even when wet — without looking rough or industrial.

Comfort Height Toilets

A few inches taller than standard — dramatically easier on knees, hips, and back. A small change that makes a real daily difference.

Wider Doorways & Turning Space

Wide enough for a walker or wheelchair. We handle framing, finishing, and any load-bearing considerations so the opening is safe and clean, not a patch job.
